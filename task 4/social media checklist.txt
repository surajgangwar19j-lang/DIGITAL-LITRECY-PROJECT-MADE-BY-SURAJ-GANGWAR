PART B
SOCIAL MEDIA DO'S AND DON'TS FOR COLLEGE STUDENTS

--- DO'S ---
1. Set your personal social media accounts to private.
2. Use a professional headshot for your LinkedIn profile.
3. Share your coding projects, certifications, and academic wins.
4. Fact-check information before sharing it with your network.
5. Network actively with alumni and industry leaders in your field.

--- DON'TS ---
1. Don't post confidential university information or exam content.
2. Don't engage in "flaming" or aggressive online arguments.
3. Don't post photos depicting illegal or unprofessional behavior.
4. Don't use offensive language, slurs, or hate speech.
5. Don't overshare your real-time location or sensitive personal data.
