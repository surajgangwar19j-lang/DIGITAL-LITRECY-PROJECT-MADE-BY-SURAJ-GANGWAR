PROJECT REPORT

TASK 1
Project Report: Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic
For this task, I used Canva to design a comprehensive one-page infographic tailored for my fellow students. My objective was to create a visual resource that simplifies complex digital concepts into actionable advice. While the assignment required covering at least three topics, I decided to include all five recommended areas—defining digital literacy, essential student tools, internet safety, maintaining a professional online presence, and mastering email etiquette—to provide a complete guide.

Regarding the design, I utilized a "vertical flow" layout to guide the reader through each section. I selected a high-contrast, futuristic color palette to make the information visually engaging and modern. To ensure the resource was easily scannable, I paired each section with thematic icons and concise, bulleted text.

The most interesting part of this process was distilling broad concepts like "digital literacy" and "online presence" into impactful, short sentences. The most difficult part was managing the space to ensure the design didn't feel cluttered. This exercise helped me realize that effective communication is as much about clarity and brevity as it is about the visual presentation.

TASK 4
PROJECT REPORT: DIGITAL COMMUNICATION AND ETIQUETTE

Poor digital communication can lead to significant professional setbacks. A common scenario involves a student sending an overly casual email to a professor, failing to include a clear subject line or formal greeting. In a real-world situation, a student might miss a critical deadline because their informal request was buried in the professor's inbox or perceived as disrespectful and ignored. 

To prevent this, the student should have used a descriptive subject line and a professional salutation. A clear structure and a polite tone ensure that the recipient understands the request immediately and feels inclined to help. Practicing professional etiquette in every digital interaction—whether via email or social media—is essential for building a reliable reputation. These tasks helped me refine my ability to communicate with authority figures and represent myself professionally across different digital platforms.

TASK 5
PROJECT REPORT: CYBERCRIME AWARENESS

While researching UPI fraud, I was most surprised by how simple psychological manipulation is used to bypass advanced technical security. The fact that many people are tricked into entering their PIN to "receive" money shows that human error, not system failure, is often the weakest link. 

As a result of this research, one habit I will personally change is the way I handle QR codes from unknown sources. I will now double-check the recipient's name in the UPI app before confirming any transaction and will strictly avoid scanning codes sent by strangers on marketplaces. Ensuring that I am a "skeptical user" is the best way to prevent falling victim to these evolving digital threats.
