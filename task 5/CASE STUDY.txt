CASE STUDY: UPI PAYMENT FRAUD (PHISHING/QR CODE SCAM)

1. WHAT IS THE CRIME?
UPI (Unified Payments Interface) fraud involves deceiving victims into sending money or granting access to their bank accounts through malicious links or QR codes.

2. STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS:
Step 1: The attacker lists a fake product on a secondary marketplace or sends a message claiming the victim has won a prize.
Step 2: The attacker contacts the victim and sends a QR code via WhatsApp, claiming it is for "receiving" a payment.
Step 3: The victim, unaware that entering a UPI PIN is only for sending money, scans the code and enters their PIN.
Step 4: The transaction is instantly authorized, and funds are deducted from the victim's account and moved to a mule account.

3. WHO IS TARGETED?
College students and young adults are frequent targets because they use UPI daily for small transactions and often participate in online buying/selling.

4. CONSEQUENCES:
The primary consequence is immediate financial loss. Since UPI transactions are instantaneous and irreversible, recovering the funds is extremely difficult. Additionally, victims may suffer from emotional distress and a loss of trust in digital payment systems.
