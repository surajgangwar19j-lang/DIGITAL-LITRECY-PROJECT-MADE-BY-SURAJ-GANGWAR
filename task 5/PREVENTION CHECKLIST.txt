'STAY SAFE ONLINE' PREVENTION CHECKLIST

1. Never enter your UPI PIN to "receive" money. PINs are only for sending/paying.
2. Verify the identity of anyone sending payment links or QR codes on WhatsApp.
3. Use different, strong passwords for your banking apps and social media.
4. Enable biometric or two-factor authentication (2FA) on all financial apps.
5. Avoid using public Wi-Fi (like at cafes or stations) for bank transactions.
6. Regularly monitor your bank statements for any unauthorized small deductions.
7. Don't click on "urgent" links in SMS messages claiming your account is blocked.
8. Report any fraud immediately to the National Cyber Crime Portal.

REPORTING CHANNELS:
- Website: cybercrime.gov.in
- Helpline: 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline)
